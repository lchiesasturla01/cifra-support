Cifra Privacy Policy

Updated November 7, 2026.

Cifra: Hidden Photo Vault is an iPhone calculator with a local private photo gallery. The developer responsible for this app is Lucas Nicolas Chiesa Sturla. Contact: lucaschiesa01@gmail.com.

Photos and local data

Imported photos, albums, your passcode, math notes and calculator history stay on your device. Cifra does not upload your photos, passcode, notes or calculations to our servers or to RevenueCat. The vault is excluded from system backups. We do not offer cloud synchronization or passcode recovery.

You choose photos through Apple's photo picker. If you choose to delete originals after importing them, the app requests photo-library access and iOS asks you to authorize deletion. Face ID is handled by iOS: Cifra receives an authentication result and does not access your biometric data.

Purchases and RevenueCat

Apple processes payments. We do not receive your card number or bank details. RevenueCat verifies purchases, restores them and determines access to Cifra Pro. It processes an automatically generated app user identifier, transaction identifiers and receipts, purchased products, subscription status and dates, and technical information about the app, device and network requests. This information supports purchase functionality and subscription analytics.

See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy and Apple's Privacy Policy.

Currency conversion

The currency feature retrieves exchange rates from ExchangeRate-API through open.er-api.com. The provider receives ordinary network-request information such as your IP address. Cifra does not send photos, passcodes, calculations, notes or amounts you enter. Exchange rates are cached on your device. See the provider's terms and privacy information.

No advertising or advertising tracking

Cifra has no ads and does not use Apple's advertising identifier. We do not sell your data or use your photos for advertising or AI model training.

Retention, deletion and your choices

You can delete photos, albums, notes and calculation history within the app. Deleting the app deletes its local files but does not cancel subscriptions or delete purchase records that Apple and RevenueCat must retain. Contact the developer for purchase-data access or deletion requests. Verification using transaction information may be necessary; never send your password, passcode or private photos.

Service providers may process data outside your country according to their policies and legal obligations. They retain records needed to operate the service, prevent fraud and comply with the law. This policy will be updated if features or data practices change.
