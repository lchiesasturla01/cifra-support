Cifra: Hidden Photo Vault

An everyday calculator with a private space for your photos.

Need help?

Contact lucaschiesa01@gmail.com with your iPhone model, iOS version and a description of the issue. Never send your passcode or private photos.

Open your vault

Enter your passcode in the calculator, then tap equals (=). Face ID is available from the passcode screen. To change your passcode, hold AC and confirm your current code.

Purchases and free trial

The calculator is free. Cifra Pro lets you import photos into your private gallery and organize albums. Monthly and yearly subscriptions include a 7-day trial for eligible customers. Lifetime access is a one-time purchase without a free trial. Apple displays the final price and eligibility before you buy.

Subscriptions renew automatically until cancelled. Cancel at least 24 hours before your trial or current period ends in Apple Account subscription settings. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. To restore a purchase, open Cifra Pro from the calculator menu and tap Restore Purchases using the same Apple Account.

Your photos and passcode

Imported photos stay in the app on your iPhone, without a cloud backup. Deleting originals from Photos leaves the imported copies in Cifra. Deleting Cifra or losing your device can permanently lose your vault. Forgotten passcodes cannot be recovered. Export photos you want to keep before deleting the app.

If Pro expires, existing photos remain available to view, export and delete. New imports require Pro.
